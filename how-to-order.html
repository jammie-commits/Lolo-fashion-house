A fit that feels like you

Take your measurements.

Use a flexible tape, keep it comfortably snug and ask someone to help for accuracy. The studio will confirm which measurements are needed for your design.

Illustration only — not a medical or tailoring scale.

Bust: around the fullest part of the chest.

Waist: around the natural waist.

Hips: around the fullest part of the hips.

Height: standing straight, without shoes.

Shoulder to hem: from shoulder point to desired garment length.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Can I request alterations?

Ask the studio about fitting and alteration options for your design before confirming an order.

Do you accept rush orders?

Availability depends on the design and event date. Contact the studio to discuss your timing before placing an order.

Can I change the fabric or colour?

Customisation may be possible depending on fabric availability and timing. Confirm options and any price changes with the studio.

What is your refund or cancellation policy?

Review the order terms provided with your quote and ask the studio to clarify cancellation, refund or remake details before you confirm.

How should I care for my garment?

Care instructions depend on the fabric and embellishments. Follow the garment's final care label and ask the studio before washing or pressing.
