Our Story

From a sewing machine in the sitting room to a full atelier floor.

LoLo Fashion House started small — one seamstress, one machine, and a growing list of neighbours and family asking for one more dress, one more suit. Every piece was cut, fitted and finished by hand.

That same care now runs through a full production house. We still measure, cut and bead every garment ourselves — we've just gotten better at doing it for more people, more ceremonies, and more countries.

Today we dress entire bridal parties for weddings and ruracio ceremonies across Kenya, and ship completed, made-to-measure pieces to clients in the diaspora.

The Beginning

One machine, made to order

Custom pieces sewn by hand for family, friends, and word-of-mouth clients — the start of a reputation built on fit and finish.

Growing the Craft

A full bridal & occasion-wear house

Wedding gowns, ruracio sets, ankara, senator suits and bridesmaid dresses, each made to individual measurements.

Today

Worldwide delivery, hand-finished as ever

An atelier and production floor dressing clients in Kenya and the diaspora — no stress, no uncertainty, custom-made either way.
