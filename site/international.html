How it works

From first message to delivery.

Every order is different, so we guide you through each step personally.

1. Enquire

Message us on WhatsApp with your occasion, location and the design you have in mind.

2. Design consultation and approval

We discuss fabric, style and details, and you approve the design before work begins.

3. Measurements

For custom clothing we guide you through the measurements we need and how to take them accurately.

4. Production timeline

Timelines depend on the design and our schedule. We confirm the timeline with you when we agree your order.

5. Shipping and delivery

Shipping arrangements and delivery estimates are confirmed per order and destination.

6. Payment, customs and import charges

We confirm payment options with you. Customs or import charges may apply in your country and are usually the customer’s responsibility; ask us if you are unsure.

7. Returns and alterations

Policies on returns and alterations for made-to-order items are discussed before you order. Please ask us for the details that apply to your order.
