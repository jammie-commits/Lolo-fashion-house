A Guide to Choosing the Perfect Custom Wedding Outfit

How to choose a custom wedding outfit for the bride, groom and wedding party, from fabric and fit to finishing details.

Know your venue and season

An outdoor daytime wedding calls for different fabrics from an evening reception. Tell us where and when so we can recommend suitable materials.

Prioritise fit

Custom tailoring is made around you. Allow time for fittings so small adjustments can be made before the day.

Think about the whole look

Consider accessories, hair, shoes and how your outfit sits beside your partner’s and your party’s.

Keep a little room for comfort

You will sit, dance and hug many people. Choose a design you will enjoy wearing all day.
