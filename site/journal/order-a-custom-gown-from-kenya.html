How to Order a Custom-Made Gown from Kenya, Wherever You Are

A simple guide to ordering a made-to-measure gown from LoLo Fashion House when you live outside Kenya: from first enquiry to measurements and approval.

Start with your occasion

Tell us what you are celebrating, the date, and the look you have in mind. Inspiration photos, preferred colours and fabrics help us understand your vision from the very first message.

Share accurate measurements

A made-to-measure gown only fits as well as the measurements behind it. We will guide you on which measurements to take and how to take them. Ask someone to help, wear fitted clothing, and send photos if you are unsure.

Approve the design before work begins

We discuss the design with you and confirm the details before production. This is the time to ask about fabric, finishing and any cultural or religious requirements.

Agree on delivery together

Delivery arrangements, timelines and any customs or import charges depend on your location. Contact our team and we will confirm these for your order before you commit.
