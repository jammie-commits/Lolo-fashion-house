Everything You Need for a Beautiful Wedding Morning

A checklist for the wedding morning: robes, hangers, getting-ready details and personalised keepsakes for the bride and her party.

Set the scene

A calm, well-prepared room makes for relaxed photographs. Choose a space with good natural light and enough room for your bridesmaids.

Gather the details

Personalised hangers, a ring mirror and champagne glasses give the photographer beautiful details to capture, and keepsakes to treasure afterwards.

Dress the party

Matching robes and props for your bridesmaids keep the morning cohesive and fun.

Let someone else hold the plan

A bridal assistant can keep the morning on schedule so that you can enjoy it.
